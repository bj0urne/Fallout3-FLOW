=== Short description (under 350 words) ===

GFO (Gameplay Feel Overhaul) makes moving, aiming and looking around in Fallout 3 feel like a modern game, in both third and first person.

In third person your legs face where you're going, your character turns in place with planted feet, strafing keeps your aim steady, starts and stops ease in and out, and landings no longer lock you in a stumble. Free look lets you look around while your character stands still.

The camera glides smoothly between first and third person, zooms smoothly with the mouse wheel, and can swap shoulders on a key.

A dynamic crosshair shows your gun's real accuracy, pulses with every shot and closes while you reload, and your shots go where it points, even up close in third person.

In first person, the sway system gives your gun weight: it trails behind your view, bobs with your steps, dips on jumps and landings, and reacts to strafing, stairs and crouching.

No custom skeleton, no animation replacers, no ESP and no scripts. Every feature can be turned off or tuned in GFO.ini.

Requires the Fallout Anniversary Patcher (game version 1.7.0.3) and FOSE. Recommended with T.E.M.P.O. for high framerates.
