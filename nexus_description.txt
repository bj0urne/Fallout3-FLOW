[center][b][color=#d4d4d8][size=6]FLOW[/size][/color][/b][/center]
[center][color=#D4D4D8][size=4]Player Control Overhaul (Not an acronym, FLOW just sounds cool)[/size]

Makes moving, aiming and looking around in Fallout 3 feel smooth and modern, in both first and third person.[/color][/center]


[line]


[center][color=#D4D4D8][b][size=5]What is this?[/size][/b][/color][/center]
[color=#D4D4D8]
FLOW is a new (AI composed) FOSE plugin that reworks how your character moves, aims and looks. No custom skeleton, no animation replacers, no ESP, no scripts. Every feature can be turned off or tuned in [i]FLOW.ini[/i].[/color]


[line]


[center][color=#D4D4D8][b][size=5]Features[/size][/b][/color][/center]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[size=4][b]Third person[/b][/size][/color]

[list]
[*][b]Natural movement:[/b] your body faces the direction you're moving. Turning while moving makes you lean into turns and your body lags behind slightly to give a natural feeling to movement. With a weapon drawn, turning around while still only turns your torso, until you turn far enough at which your legs also turn.[/*]
[*][b]Vertical smoothness:[/b] the camera follows your height with a smooth delay, so on a jump you rise and drop ahead of it.[/*]
[*][b]Free look (weapon holstered):[/b] camera can be moved around freely, and your character moves his head to look at what you're aiming at. Camera now naturally orbits the player instead of orbiting the crosshair as in the vanilla game, finally fixing vanity camera.[/*]
[*][b]Acceleration:[/b] movement easing. Starting and stopping is smooth.[/*]
[*][b]No clunky movement:[/b] landing hard no longer locks you into a stumble animation. You're free to land and instantly move.[/*]
[*][b]Shoulder swap[/b] you can now swap shoulder for the camera in third person on a key (X by default).[/*]
[/list]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[size=4][b]First person[/b][/size][/color]

[list]
[*][color=#D4D4D8][b]Gun sway system:[/b] this mod features a sway system for your first person viewmodels. Looking around moves the weapon like it actually weighs something, moving bobs the weapon using the same sway system, and jumping/landing dips the weapon to give a sense of weight. All of these combined using the same system make gunplay feel much more fluid than the base game.[/color][/*]
[/list]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[size=4][b]Crosshair and aiming[/b][/size][/color]

[list]
[*][b]Dynamic crosshair:[/b] the game's own crosshair, split in two, opens up to show your gun's actual spread. The spread includes weapon accuracy, condition, skill, crippled limbs, aiming, crouching, moving and perks. Drawing a gun opens it out to your spread and fades in the centre dot; holstering closes it again. Every shot pulls it in toward the centre and back out to give a responsive feel.[/*]
[*][b]Shots follow the crosshair:[/b] now bullets actually hit what you're aiming at, New Vegas style. In the vanilla game bullets originated from the barrel of your gun, which resulted in bullets hitting walls, objects etc in front of your gun while your crosshair was clearly on a mutants head.[/*]
[/list]
[color=#D4D4D8]
[size=4][b]Other[/b][/size][/color]

[list]
[*][color=#D4D4D8][b]Smooth view switching[/b] between first and third person, and smooth zooming.[/color][/*]
[/list]

[center][color=#D4D4D8][b][size=5]Installation[/size][/b][/color][/center]
[color=#D4D4D8]
Install with Vortex or Mod Organizer 2, or extract the archive into [i]Fallout 3\Data\[/i]. Launch the game as usual with FOSE.[/color]


[line]


[center][color=#D4D4D8][b][size=5]Compatibility[/size][/b][/color][/center]


[list]
[*][color=#D4D4D8]Don't combine with other diagonal movement mods.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#D4D4D8]First person walk and jump animation replacers won't show while the sway system is on.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#D4D4D8]Uses its own crosshair. Turn it off in the INI to keep another crosshair mod.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#D4D4D8]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#D4D4D8]Fallout 3 only. Not for New Vegas or Tale of Two Wastelands.[/color][/*]
[/list]
