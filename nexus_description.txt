[center][size=6][b]FLOW[/b][/size]
[size=4]Gameplay Overhaul[/size]

Makes moving, aiming and looking around in Fallout 3 feel smooth and modern, in both first and third person.[/center]

[line]

[center][b][size=5]What is this?[/size][/b][/center]

FLOW is a new (AI composed) FOSE plugin that reworks how your character moves, aims and looks. No custom skeleton, no animation replacers, no ESP, no scripts. Every feature can be turned off or tuned in [i]FLOW.ini[/i].

[line]

[center][b][size=5]Features[/size][/b][/center]

[size=4][b]Third person[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Natural movement:[/b] your body faces the direction you're moving, leans into turns and lags slightly behind when you turn. With a weapon drawn, turning on the spot turns your torso first, and your legs follow when you turn far enough.[/*]
[*][b]Free look:[/b] with your weapon holstered, move the camera freely while your character turns their head to look where you're aiming. The camera now orbits around your character instead of the crosshair.[/*]
[*][b]Smooth starts and stops[/b] instead of instant full speed.[/*]
[*][b]No more landing stumble:[/b] land and move instantly.[/*]
[*][b]Smooth camera:[/b] the camera follows your jumps with a soft delay.[/*]
[*][b]Shoulder swap[/b] on a key (X by default).[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]First person[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Gun sway system:[/b] your weapon finally has weight. It trails behind as you look around, bobs as you move, and dips when you jump and land. Gunplay feels much more fluid than the base game.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Crosshair and aiming[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Dynamic crosshair:[/b] opens up to show your gun's real accuracy, opens when you draw and closes when you holster, and pulses with every shot.[/*]
[*][b]Shots hit what you aim at,[/b] New Vegas style. No more bullets hitting a wall in front of your gun while your crosshair is on a mutant's head.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Other[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Smooth view switching[/b] between first and third person, and smooth zooming.[/*]
[/list]

[*][b]Fallout 3[/b] or [b]Fallout 3: Game of the Year Edition[/b][/*]

[line]

[center][b][size=5]Installation[/size][/b][/center]

Install with Vortex or Mod Organizer 2, or extract the archive into [i]Fallout 3\Data\[/i]. Launch the game as usual with FOSE.

[line]

[center][b][size=5]Compatibility[/size][/b][/center]

[list]
[*]Don't combine with other diagonal movement mods.[/*]
[*]First person walk and jump animation replacers won't show while the sway system is on.[/*]
[*]Uses its own crosshair. Turn it off in the INI to keep another crosshair mod.[/*]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.[/*]
[*]Fallout 3 only. Not for New Vegas or Tale of Two Wastelands.[/*]
[/list]
